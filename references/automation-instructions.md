# Recurring-task enhancement

The recurring task is a monitoring and research process, not a holdings-news digest. Its discovery universe must remain independent of the configured portfolio.

When updating an existing market or fund automation:

1. Retrieve the exact existing task and its identifier.
2. Preserve its schedule, enabled state, title unless a change is necessary, configured holdings, delivery behavior, and all unrelated instructions.
3. Merge the enhancement below into the existing prompt. Do not replace unknown existing requirements and do not create a second task.
4. Verify the updated task after the write.

Do not update the live task until the revised skill has passed structural and representative behavioral tests. If the active task is local to another machine and cannot be accessed, prepare the exact merged prompt and installation package without claiming the live task was changed.

The recurring task must also pass data-engine acceptance on the target machine. Before release, configure and test the packaged engine described in `data-engine.md`; do not treat the presence of connector code as proof that endpoints, API entitlements, timestamps, units, or sessions are usable.

## Enhancement block

Generate a three-layer evidence-first daily report: a one-minute view, complete independent market research, and a separate personalized impact appendix.

Before writing the report, run the configured local data-engine `allocation` profile, then its `health` and `snapshot` commands. During or after the mainland-China session, also run the `tactical` profile when a tactical question, configured watchlist, unusual breadth, or risk trigger makes it relevant. Retain the structured snapshot as the numerical evidence base; use web research for official releases, filings, announcements, methodology, and context.

If a required profile is not configured, an API key is absent, a provider fails or returns no usable rows, or the newest observation belongs to the wrong session, mark that dataset unavailable and lower the affected conclusion. Never fill the gap from memory, an undated search result, a related index, or a differently defined vendor field. Do not claim the recurring system is fully deployed until both profiles required by its configuration have passed five consecutive trading-session checks on the target machine.

Distinguish raw/provider-reported data, locally calculated indicators, provider-derived flow or trade-direction labels, and model interpretation. MACD, RSI, ATR, volume ratio, breadth, order-book imbalance, inner/outer volume, and BBD/DDE-like flow are supporting signals. None independently proves valuation, earnings improvement, causal policy transmission, institutional ownership, or expected return.

Apply a holdings firewall. During the independent research and opportunity-discovery stages, ignore the configured holdings as an information filter. Lock the market theses, risks, sector conclusions, and opportunity matrix before beginning personalization. The holdings may change portfolio fit, urgency, or implementation, but must not cause unheld opportunities to disappear or the market conclusion to be rewritten.

Begin with a one-minute view containing exactly the three most important developments, their changed expectations and cross-asset implications, and whether the previous core thesis strengthened, weakened, remained unchanged, or failed. When the previous report is accessible, explicitly compare its thesis, scenarios, watch items, and invalidation conditions with new evidence. Carry unresolved items forward. If no reliable previous report is accessible, say that today's report establishes the baseline.

Always include a visibly separate previous-view verification section. On the first accessible run, put the baseline statement there rather than only mentioning it in the executive view.

Use an append-only thesis ledger when the task environment supports durable state. Preserve each thesis's original cutoff, wording, horizon, benchmark, confirmation condition, and invalidation condition. Append dated updates; never rewrite the old entry using later outcomes. Classify affected views as `strengthened`, `unchanged`, `weakened`, `invalidated`, `not yet testable`, or `expired`.

When a run establishes the baseline, persist complete ledger entries for only the one to three material theses using the thesis-ledger schema. The visible previous-view table is only a summary and does not replace stable thesis IDs, expected observations, benchmarks, alternatives, and action relevance.

Then complete a full market analysis that is independent of the user's current holdings. Do not use the portfolio as a filter for what markets, assets, policies, risks, or opportunities deserve coverage. Analyze material developments across mainland China, Hong Kong, the United States, global macro and policy, rates and credit, currencies, gold and other important commodities, earnings, valuation, liquidity, flows, and sentiment. Prioritize the central market narrative and causal relationships rather than listing news. For each major conclusion, distinguish confirmed facts from interpretation, explain the transmission channel and time horizon, test a competing explanation, and identify evidence that would invalidate the view. State the current market regime, plausible forward scenarios, risks, and upcoming watch items. Include important areas even when the user does not hold them.

Scan the full relevant industry and theme universe each day for breadth, relative leadership, earnings revisions, valuation shifts, policy and supply-chain catalysts, and reliable flow or positioning changes. For mainland China, Hong Kong, and the United States, include one compact row naming strengthening and weakening industries or themes, one-day/one-week/one-to-three-month evidence, breadth or concentration, confirming or conflicting evidence, rotation state, and confidence. A broad regional index or macro label is not a sector result. If evidence is missing, identify the missing window or evidence type instead of omitting the market. Do not reproduce a raw sector leaderboard. Deep-dive only sectors with a material change or those becoming a market-leading theme. Distinguish durable rotation from a one-day rebound, short covering, or low-liquidity noise.

Include a separate opportunity matrix covering opportunities whether held or not. For every candidate, state one opportunity type (`fundamental improvement`, `policy or liquidity transmission`, `valuation mean reversion with catalyst`, or `defensive or diversification value`), why now, supporting evidence, confidence (`high`, `medium`, or `low`), catalyst, horizon, main risk, confirmation and invalidation conditions, a credible alternative or reason waiting is preferable, a concrete investable path with researched security or exact product/share codes, and exactly one follow-up status: `worth dedicated research`, `watch`, or `not yet`. Complete material due diligence for promising named candidates within the authorized autonomous research scope, including product comparisons when they affect the choice. Research status is an intermediate gate; finish with a justified build/add/hold/reduce/exit/wait recommendation and operational conditions. Keep the report compact, and leave execution to the user.

Apply a hard evidence gate to every opportunity. Two articles about one event, correlated price moves, or a headline plus its same-session price response are one event cluster, not independent confirmation. `worth dedicated research` requires at least two independent evidence types and no material data-contract gap; `watch` requires two independent evidence types but still awaits one named trigger; use `not yet` when only one event cluster exists, evidence conflicts, or a required window or field is missing. A `watch` or `not yet` state implies no investment action. Populate every matrix field, and keep cash, money-market, and duration exposures separate when their mechanisms differ.

Opportunity discovery is cross-asset rather than stock-only. Consider bonds, cash, currencies, commodities, styles, regions, sectors, indices, funds, ETFs, QDII, and individual securities when supported by the evidence. If no candidate reaches `worth dedicated research`, output `no opportunity cleared the dedicated-research gate; N watch items remain`. If none reaches even `watch`, output `no qualified opportunity or watch item`. Never invent a candidate to appear proactive.

For the strongest actionable or watchlist thesis, run an adversarial review: present the best supporting case, the strongest contrary evidence and competing explanation, and a risk-manager judgment comparing action with the best alternative and waiting. Rhetorical confidence does not substitute for evidence.

When the user proposes an action, choose exactly one labeled `Exposure stance:` from `reject for now`, `watch, no action yet`, `worth dedicated research`, or `review threshold reached`. A non-action stance must not be softened with vague sizing language. Compare the proposed action, a credible alternative, the existing plan, and waiting in one compact table. Put confirmation, review, and invalidation conditions in one trigger register with required columns for metric or event, source, direction or threshold, observation window, and next review point. Use numeric cutoffs only when a defensible baseline exists; otherwise use a specific event rule. An undefined term such as `improved`, `continued`, or `weakened` makes the trigger `not operational` and unable to support promotion.

Only after the independent market report is complete, add a clearly labeled section titled `对我当前持仓的影响`. Preserve and use the holdings already configured in this task or an authoritative portfolio ledger; do not invent missing positions. Map the market conclusions to each holding through its underlying index or asset, geography, sector, currency, rate sensitivity, and product structure. Separate direct and indirect effects and short-, medium-, and long-term implications. Then assess portfolio-level concentration, overlap, currency exposure, correlation, diversification, recurring-investment risk, and whether the original investment thesis changed. End with one separately namespaced `Portfolio status:`: `原计划不变`, `重点观察`, or `达到复核阈值`, with evidence. This portfolio-review status must not replace or loosen the locked exposure stance. Do not turn a one-day move or a single headline into a buy or sell instruction.

State the analysis cutoff and timezone. Prefer official releases and filings, exchanges and index providers, established market-data vendors, and then reputable financial reporting; use aggregators only as disclosed fallbacks. For historical-cutoff runs, exclude later retrospectives, revised information unavailable then, and all post-cutoff outcomes. Cite material facts with direct, accessible links, disclose uncertainty and stale or missing data, and never fabricate prices, returns, sector breadth, flows, holdings, forecasts, or sources.

For material observations, verify applicable timestamps, market sessions, units, currencies, adjustment basis, publication or revision timing, and latency. When sources conflict, disclose the conflict and lower confidence rather than selecting the value that best supports the narrative.

Control report length with a single-home rule: the one-minute view is the canonical home for its three headline observations; later sections cite their evidence IDs and add analysis without repeating the same exact levels or releases. The dashboard states changes, the narrative interprets them, and the causal table tests only the most important theses. On quiet days, state when the regime or sector picture did not materially change and keep deep dives and the opportunity matrix short. Expand only sections affected by material events.

Before delivery, verify the collection health and snapshot cutoff, reapply the opportunity-type core-evidence and target-identity gates, verify every Markdown table row matches its header, check the trigger register for empty or undefined fields, persist any new baseline ledger entries, and replace repeated headline values outside the one-minute view with evidence IDs.
